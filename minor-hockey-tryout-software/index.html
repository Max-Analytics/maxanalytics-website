How Max runs your evaluations and tryouts

Each step replaces work that volunteers usually do by hand, from the first schedule email to the last placement conversation.

  1. Import your players

    Import a spreadsheet from Hockey Canada Registry or any registration system. Max matches the columns and sorts players into their age groups.

  2. Schedule and notify families

    Max suggests a best-practice format for the number of players, schedules the evaluation ice times and emails or texts every family their times.

  3. Check in at the arena

    Take attendance as players arrive and assign jersey numbers and colours, so evaluators know exactly who is on the ice.

  4. Score in real time

    Every evaluator scores each skill in each drill on a phone or tablet. If the arena Wi-Fi drops, scores are saved on the device and sync later.

  5. Rank players fairly

    Max weights and combines all evaluators' scores and updates UNBIAS™ rankings within minutes of each ice time. Schedule follow-up skates for players on the bubble.

  6. Place teams and send report cards

    Assign players to teams, notify families of their level or team, and send every player a report card. Coaches start the season with a baseline.

Frequently asked questions

What is minor hockey evaluation and tryout software?

It replaces paper score sheets and spreadsheets for an association's evaluations and tryouts. Evaluators score players on a phone or tablet during each ice time, the software combines every evaluator's scores into rankings, and the association uses those rankings to place players on teams. Max also schedules the ice times, notifies families, runs check-in and sends each player a report card.

Can we import players from Hockey Canada Registry?

Yes. Export your registered players from Hockey Canada Registry, or any registration system, as a spreadsheet and import it into Max. Max matches the columns for you and keeps each player's Hockey Canada ID with their record.

Can we use our own evaluation format and drills?

Yes. Max suggests a best-practice format based on how many players you are evaluating, and you can replace it with your own format, drills and skill weightings. Your account manager sets this up with you.

What devices do evaluators need?

Each evaluator needs a phone or tablet with a web browser. Max does not supply devices.

What happens if the arena Wi-Fi drops during an evaluation?

Evaluators keep scoring. Scores are saved on the device and sent to Max when the connection comes back.

How much does Max cost?

Max is priced per evaluated player per year: $8 CAD for Evaluate, $12 CAD for Develop and $16 CAD for Compete. Every plan includes unlimited administrators and evaluators.

Do players and parents get feedback?

Yes. Max automatically produces a personalized report card for every evaluated player, showing how they compared with their peers and where to focus next.

How do we get started?

It starts with a planning call to understand your evaluations. Max then guides you through setup, trains a super-user and supports your first evaluation.
