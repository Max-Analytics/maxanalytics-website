The papers are short, plain, and free. They are meant to start conversations, not close them.

Association boards

Who set team counts from this year's registration numbers and answer the emails in November.

Evaluators and evaluation leads

Who give their September evenings and want their judgment to count fairly.

Regional tier-setters

Who apply one structure across associations that are never quite alike.

Parents and coaches

Who recognize the quiet room after a 7 to 0 game, in either direction.
