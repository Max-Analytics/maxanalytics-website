Max Analytics vs TeamGenius for minor hockey tryouts

TeamGenius and Max Analytics both replace paper score sheets for minor hockey evaluations. TeamGenius is a multi-sport evaluation platform priced in annual tiers by number of athletes, with some tryout features on its Pro and Elite plans or available as add-ons. Max is built for minor hockey associations: it adjusts scores for each evaluator’s tendencies automatically, emails and texts families their ice times and results, and includes check-in, jerseys and attendance on every plan.

Where Max goes further

Evaluator tendencies handled automatically

Some evaluators score high and some score low. UNBIAS™ compares each evaluator’s scores on each skill with the panel’s, adjusts for consistently lenient or tough scoring once there is enough evidence, and accounts for which evaluators each player drew. TeamGenius’s help centre describes filtering results by evaluator and setting evaluator weights manually.

Email and text to every family

Max sends evaluation times, reminders, schedule changes, and level or team results by email, text or both, and families can confirm whether they’re coming. Coordinators stop answering the same schedule questions one email at a time.

Tryout-day essentials on every plan

Check-in with jersey numbers and colours, walk-ons, attendance, family messaging and bias-adjusted rankings are all part of Max’s first plan, so there is nothing extra to add before evaluation night.

A hockey format that adapts between skates

On the Develop and Compete plans, Max’s automated workflow starts from hockey evaluation formats for U9 to U18, sized to how many players you have, then regroups players between skates by ranking, with byes and holds for the players on the bubble.

Frequently asked questions

Is Max an alternative to TeamGenius for minor hockey?

Yes. Max suits associations that want evaluator tendencies adjusted automatically, families reached by email and text, and check-in and attendance on every plan. It is built specifically for minor hockey associations.

How does Max handle evaluator bias?

Max’s UNBIAS™ rankings adjust scores for each evaluator’s lenient or tough tendencies automatically, once there is enough scoring to measure them. TeamGenius documents filtering by evaluator and setting evaluator weights.

Can we move our players from TeamGenius to Max?

Yes. TeamGenius offers a full data export. Export your players as a spreadsheet and import it into Max, which matches the columns for you.

Does Max text families?

Yes. Max sends evaluation times, reminders, schedule changes and results by email, text or both.
