Max Analytics vs SkillShark for minor hockey tryouts

SkillShark is a multi-sport athlete evaluation app used across more than 30 sports, priced per athlete in US dollars. Max Analytics is built for minor hockey associations and runs the whole evaluation around the scoring: it emails and texts families their ice times and results, checks players in with jerseys on every plan, and adjusts rankings for each evaluator’s tendencies with UNBIAS™.

Where Max goes further

The whole evaluation, not just scoring night

Scoring is one night of a process that starts weeks earlier. Max schedules ice times, notifies families, checks players in and assigns jerseys, then ranks, places and reports, all in one place.

Families reached by email and text

Evaluation times, reminders, schedule changes and results go out by email, text or both, and families can confirm attendance, so the coordinator isn’t chasing replies.

Evaluator tendencies, not just outliers

SkillShark’s Score Audit flags single scores that look unusually high or low. Max’s UNBIAS™ works at the evaluator level: it measures whether each evaluator tends to score high or low on each skill and adjusts for it once there is enough evidence.

Check-in, messaging and rankings in the first plan

Max’s first plan, $8 CAD per player, includes check-in with jersey numbers and colours, walk-ons, attendance, family email and text, and bias-adjusted rankings. At SkillShark, check-in and messaging start on the All-Star plan at $10 USD per athlete.

Built around hockey associations

On the Develop and Compete plans, Max’s automated workflow starts from hockey evaluation formats for U9 to U18, sized to how many players you have, then regroups players between skates by ranking, with byes and holds for the players on the bubble.

Frequently asked questions

Is Max an alternative to SkillShark for minor hockey?

Yes. Max suits associations that want one system for the whole evaluation: scheduling, family email and text, check-in with jerseys, bias-adjusted rankings and report cards, priced in Canadian dollars.

How does pricing compare?

Max charges $8, $12 or $16 CAD per evaluated player per year, with check-in and family messaging on every plan. SkillShark lists $5, $10 or $15 USD per athlete per year, with check-in and messaging starting on the $10 plan.

Can we move our players from SkillShark to Max?

Yes. Export your players from SkillShark to Excel and import the spreadsheet into Max, which matches the columns for you.
