Minor hockey tryout and evaluation software compared

Max Analytics, TeamGenius and SkillShark are evaluation apps that minor hockey associations use for tryouts, and all three score players digitally. They differ in how much of the evaluation they run for you: Max is built for minor hockey associations and handles scheduling, family email and text, check-in with jerseys, rankings adjusted for evaluator tendencies and a report card for every player, with published prices in Canadian dollars.

Where Max goes further

Evaluator tendencies adjusted for you

UNBIAS™ measures whether each evaluator tends to score high or low on each skill, adjusts for it once there is enough evidence, and accounts for which evaluators each player drew. No one sets evaluator weights by hand.

Families kept informed

Evaluation times, reminders, schedule changes and results go to families by email, text or both, and families can confirm whether they’re coming.

Tryout-day essentials on every plan

Check-in with jersey numbers and colours, walk-ons, attendance, family messaging and bias-adjusted rankings are all part of Max’s first plan.

A hockey format that adapts between skates

On the Develop and Compete plans, Max’s automated workflow starts from hockey evaluation formats for U9 to U18, sized to how many players you have, then regroups players between skates by ranking, with byes and holds for the players on the bubble.

Frequently asked questions

What should a minor hockey association look for in tryout software?

Consistent scoring across evaluators, scoring that keeps working without Wi-Fi, check-in with jerseys, messages to families, rankings you can explain, and feedback for every player. Max Analytics is built specifically for minor hockey associations and covers each of these, from scheduling and family messages to bias-adjusted rankings and report cards.

Which hockey tryout software adjusts for evaluator bias?

Max Analytics adjusts scores for each evaluator’s tendencies automatically with UNBIAS™. TeamGenius documents filtering by evaluator and setting evaluator weights, and SkillShark flags individual unusual scores.

Which tryout software lists prices in Canadian dollars?

Max Analytics lists its prices in Canadian dollars: $8, $12 or $16 per evaluated player per year. TeamGenius lists US-dollar prices and offers billing in Canadian dollars. SkillShark lists US-dollar prices.

What about all-in-one platforms like Playbook?

Playbook (callplaybook.com) runs registration, payments, scheduling and websites for a whole sports organization, with a tryout module. Max focuses on hockey evaluations and works alongside your registration platform. See Max vs Playbook.
