Where Max goes further

Specialised for hockey evaluations

Max does one job: evaluations and tryouts for minor hockey associations. Formats, drills, weightings, check-in and reports are designed around ice times, age groups, levels and teams.

Evaluator tendencies adjusted automatically

With several evaluators on the ice, some will score high and some low. Max’s UNBIAS™ measures each evaluator’s tendencies on each skill and adjusts rankings for them, so no single evaluator decides a placement.

A report card for every player

Every evaluated player gets a report card showing how they compared with their peers, so families see what was assessed and where to focus next.

Scoring that survives arena Wi-Fi

If the connection drops mid-skate, evaluators keep scoring. Scores are saved on the device and sent to Max when the connection returns.

Using Max alongside Playbook

Playbook and Max do different jobs. Playbook runs registration, payments, scheduling and websites for a whole sports organization. Max runs your hockey evaluations and tryouts. Associations can keep the registration platform they already use and import their registered players into Max by spreadsheet.
